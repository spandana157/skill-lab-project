PROJECT TITLE-STUDENT EXPENSE TRACKER
Problem Statement:
Students often find it difficult to manage their daily expenses because they do not maintain proper records of their spending.
Small expenses on food, transportation, shopping, and other activities can accumulate without their knowledge.
This makes it difficult to understand where their money is being spent and whether they are staying within their budget.
The Student Expense Tracker is designed to provide a simple and efficient way for students to record, categorize, and
monitor their expenses. The system helps students understand their spending habits, track their budget, and 
make better financial decisions.
  Prototype:
A prototype is a basic model of the proposed system that shows how the application will work.
  Proposed Solution:
The proposed solution is a user-friendly expense management application that helps students record and monitor their spending in one place.

The system allows users to enter their expenses with details such as amount, category, date, and description.
These records are stored in a database and can be viewed whenever required. The application calculates total expenses and compares 
them with the user’s monthly budget
